Load a sample or paste a brief, then hit Analyse trip. You will get a body-clock plan per day, a five-axis scorecard, ranked findings with fixes, and a prioritised action list.

  • Real circadian math. Two-process sleep model with melatonin gating, a sleep-maintenance-zone awakening, and hour-by-hour adaptation.
  • Direction aware. Eastward and westward shifts get opposite light, meal and caffeine advice, because they need opposite corrections.
  • Event aware. Meetings are scored against your body clock, so a 10:00 local meeting that lands at 03:00 on your internal clock gets flagged.
  • Offline. Standard library only. No API keys, no network calls, no LLM.
